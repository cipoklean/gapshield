# GapShield AI Demo Script

## Purpose
60-second video walkthrough for hackathon submission showcasing the product flow and AI visibility.

---

## 0-8s — Hook

**[Visual: Hero section, camera slowly zooms in]**

"Every weekend, US equity markets close. But tokenized stocks on Bitget trade 24 hours. Traders miss critical news and wake up to gaps they didn't prepare for."

---

## 8-20s — Problem

**[Visual: Scroll to Chrono-Sync timeline]**

"That's the weekend gap. While institutions process geopolitical events, earnings signals, and regulatory changes over Saturday and Sunday, retail traders are flying blind. By Monday open, the damage is done."

**[Key talking point: Emphasize the asymmetry — institutions have weekend desks, retail doesn't]**

---

## 20-35s — Solution

**[Visual: Scroll to Analyze section, type "TSLA" and click Generate]**

"GapShield AI solves this. Enter any ticker. Watch as the AI extracts weekend news, classifies events by market impact, cross-references historical patterns, and synthesizes a risk assessment."

**[Key talking point: Point out the 4-step pipeline animation — this is what makes it different]**

---

## 35-50s — AI Role + Evidence

**[Visual: Show the completed report with source evidence cards]**

"This isn't a black box. You see the processing pipeline and source evidence. For TSLA, the system analyzed OPEC+ headlines, EV demand sentiment, and rToken liquidity, then flagged risk with an actionable checklist."

**[Key talking point: Highlight the "AI Evidence — Data Sources" section at the bottom]**

---

## 50-60s — Close

**[Visual: Scroll back to hero, end on tagline]**

"GapShield AI does not trade for you. It serves human traders with institutional-grade intelligence, so you know Monday's risk before Monday arrives."

**[Final frame: Logo + tagline "Built for tokenized US stocks / rTokens"]**

---

## Demo Tips

1. **Type slowly** when entering the ticker — judges need to read it
2. **Pause during pipeline animation** — let them see the 4 steps complete
3. **Scroll down slowly** through the report — don't rush past the evidence cards
4. **Mention "Bitget" and "rToken"** explicitly in narration
5. **Keep it under 60 seconds** — judges watch many demos

---

## Alternative Demo Flows

### Quick Demo (30 seconds)
If time is limited, skip the hero scroll and go straight to:
- Enter ticker → Show pipeline → Show report → Exit

### Deep Dive (90 seconds)
If you have extra time:
- Explain the Chrono-Sync timeline in detail
- Show multiple tickers (AAPL, then NVDA) to demonstrate consistency
- Read aloud one source evidence card to show detail quality
